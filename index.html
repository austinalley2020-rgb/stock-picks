About

I'm Austin Alley, originally from Fort Myers, Florida, and a recent graduate of Brown University, where I played Division I football. I entered college on a pre-med track while simultaneously studying business economics, spending my first two years balancing organic chemistry, anatomy, and clinical research with coursework in microeconomic theory and corporate finance. During a gap year, I discovered that the same analytical rigor I loved in medicine (diagnosing problems, weighing evidence, forming a thesis under uncertainty) applied directly to public markets. What started as reading 10-Ks out of curiosity became a genuine obsession with fundamental equity research. This site is where I publish my highest-conviction long and short ideas.
